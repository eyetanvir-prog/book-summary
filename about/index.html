About

Written here. Not collected elsewhere.

Quarto is a catalog of 1,054 books. Every one has a short card of our own: a hook and three ideas worth keeping. Forty-seven of them also have a full essay, meant to take about four minutes: a way into the book, a few claims you can use, and the places the usual summary gets lazy. More essays are in the queue. The wording is ours. It is not a condensation scraped from another summary site, and it is not a substitute for the book.

Authors, catalog years, subject headings, short catalog descriptions, and cover images are cached from Open Library in lib/open-library.json. Those fields are theirs, including the oddities — a year that marks an edition they hold, a subject list that sounds like a card catalog. If a cover fails to load, the page keeps a designed plate instead. Nothing is stored in a database.

The sections on this site are a smaller map we made so the shelf can be browsed. They sit beside the Open Library subjects, not instead of them.
