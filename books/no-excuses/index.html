Open Library note. You dont need to have been born under a lucky star, or with incredible wealth, or with terrific contacts and connections, or even special skills ... but what you do need to succeed in any of your life goals is self-discipline. Unfortunately, most people give in to the two worst enemies of success: they take the path of least resistance (in other words, they are lazy) and/or they want immediate gratification: they dont consider the long-term consequences of the actions they take today.
