In Sheep's Clothing

A psychologist’s guide to spotting and dealing with covertly aggressive, manipulative people.

George K. SimonFirst published 1996About 172 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Simon argues manipulators are not acting from insecurity but from a desire to win and control, disguised as innocence.

  2. 02

    Common tactics include lying, guilt-tripping, playing the victim, and minimizing, all meant to keep you off balance.

  3. 03

    Protect yourself by setting firm boundaries, judging actions over words, and refusing to accept excuses.
