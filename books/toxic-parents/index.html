Toxic Parents

A therapist’s guide for adults hurt by abusive, controlling, or neglectful parents, and how to reclaim their lives.

Susan ForwardFirst published 1988About 326 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Forward describes patterns of parental harm, including control, verbal abuse, alcoholism, and physical or sexual abuse.

  2. 02

    Adults often carry guilt and self-blame, and recognizing the parent’s responsibility is part of healing.

  3. 03

    Confrontation, boundaries, and grieving lost childhood are presented as steps toward independence.
