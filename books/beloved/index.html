Bangla is on the essays

Beloved

In post-Civil War Ohio, a formerly enslaved woman’s house is haunted by her past in a way that becomes impossible to ignore.

Toni MorrisonFirst published 1987About 321 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Morrison treats slavery’s trauma as something that does not stay in the past, but returns in bodies, memories and families.

  2. 02

    The structure is fragmented and nonlinear, which asks for patience and mirrors the way trauma surfaces.

  3. 03

    It is about mothers, love and what freedom means when the past has taken so much; it is demanding and worth it.
