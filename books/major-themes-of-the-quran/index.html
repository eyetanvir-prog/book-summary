Major Themes of the Qur'an

Fazlur Rahman distills the Qur’an’s central concerns — God, humanity, nature, prophecy, and society — into a coherent vision.

Fazlur RahmanFirst published 1989About 180 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Rahman reads the Qur’an thematically, showing how its teachings form a unified moral worldview rather than scattered rules.

  2. 02

    He emphasizes justice and moral responsibility as central to the Qur’anic message, especially in its concern for social reform.

  3. 03

    A key modernist work, it invites readers to engage the text directly while appreciating its historical context.
