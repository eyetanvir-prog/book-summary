The Sun Also Rises

American and British expatriates drink their way from Paris to Pamplona, in Hemingway’s portrait of a damaged generation.

Ernest HemingwayFirst published 1926About 249 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    The characters’ restless travel and drinking show how war left them unable to settle into ordinary life.

  2. 02

    Hemingway’s spare style leaves feelings unsaid, so the tension sits in what characters avoid discussing.

  3. 03

    Its casual antisemitism toward Robert Cohn is glaring today and part of an honest reading of the book.
