Getting to Yes

The Harvard Negotiation Project’s method for reaching agreements without either giving in or digging in.

Roger Drummer FisherFirst published 1981About 200 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Separate the people from the problem, so personal feelings and relationships do not get tangled with the substance of the dispute.

  2. 02

    Focus on interests, not positions; understanding why each side wants something opens up options a fixed demand would hide.

  3. 03

    Know your best alternative to a negotiated agreement, and insist on objective criteria so outcomes rest on fairness rather than pressure.
