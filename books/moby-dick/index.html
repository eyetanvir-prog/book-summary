Moby Dick

A sailor joins a whaling ship under a captain obsessed with killing one white whale, and the book swallows everything around it.

Herman MelvilleFirst published 1851About 453 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Ahab’s obsession is the dramatic spine, a study of how a single will can drag a whole crew toward destruction.

  2. 02

    Melville digresses into whale anatomy, philosophy and the whaling business; those chapters are part of the design, not filler.

  3. 03

    Ishmael is a curious, generous narrator, and his friendship with Queequeg is one of the book’s quiet arguments against prejudice.
