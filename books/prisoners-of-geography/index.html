Prisoners of Geography

Tim Marshall explains world politics through ten maps, showing how geography constrains what nations can do.

Tim MarshallFirst published 2015About 291 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Mountains, rivers, coastlines, and plains shape national security fears and ambitions, from Russia’s need for warm-water ports to China’s borders.

  2. 02

    Geography explains why some regions remain unstable and why powers repeatedly fight over the same territory.

  3. 03

    The book is accessible and illuminating, though geography is one factor among many and should not be treated as destiny.

Open Library note. All leaders are constrained by geography. Their choices are limited by mountains, rivers, seas and concrete. Yes, to understand world events you need to understand people, ideas and movements - but if you don't know geography, you'll never have the full picture. To understand Putin's actions, for example, it is essential to consider that, to be a world power, Russia must have a navy.
