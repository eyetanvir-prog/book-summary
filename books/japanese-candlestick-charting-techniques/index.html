Japanese Candlestick Charting Techniques

The book that introduced Western traders to Japanese candlestick charts and the price patterns they reveal.

Steve NisonFirst published 1991About 307 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Each candlestick shows open, high, low, and close, making the balance between buyers and sellers visible at a glance.

  2. 02

    Patterns such as dojis, hammers, and engulfing candles can signal possible reversals or indecision when read in context.

  3. 03

    Candlesticks work best combined with Western tools like trendlines and support levels; no pattern guarantees a price move on its own.
