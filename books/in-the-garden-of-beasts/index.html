In the Garden of Beasts

An unlikely American ambassador and his daughter watch Hitler consolidate power in 1933 Berlin, mostly too slowly.

Erik LarsonFirst published 2011About 481 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Larson uses William Dodd’s family to show how ordinary diplomats and visitors explained away early Nazi violence as a phase that would pass.

  2. 02

    Dodd’s frugal, scholarly style made him an outsider in a State Department that cared more about debts and decorum than human rights.

  3. 03

    The book is a study in gradual recognition, how long it takes decent people to accept that what they are seeing is real.
