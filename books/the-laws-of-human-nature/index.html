The Laws of Human Nature

Greene’s doorstop guide to reading people, including yourself, through irrationality, envy, grandiosity, and other hidden drives.

Robert GreeneFirst published 2018About 617 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    We are governed by emotions far more than we admit, so self-awareness of your own biases is the first step to reading others.

  2. 02

    Learn to spot character over time through patterns of behavior rather than first impressions or what people say about themselves.

  3. 03

    Envy, aggression, and conformity exist in everyone, including you, and recognizing your own shadow makes you less vulnerable to it.
