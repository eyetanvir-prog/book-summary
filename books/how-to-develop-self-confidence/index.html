How to Develop Self-confidence

Carnegie’s guide to building confidence through public speaking, preparation, and practice.

Dale CarnegieFirst published 1956About 244 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Carnegie argues that confidence comes from preparation and practice, especially in speaking before groups.

  2. 02

    Speaking about what you know and care about makes you more persuasive and less nervous.

  3. 03

    Small successes compound, so face fears gradually and celebrate progress rather than expecting instant mastery.
