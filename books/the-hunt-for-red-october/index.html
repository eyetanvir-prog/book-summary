Tom ClancyFirst published 1984About 479 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    The suspense is in inference: analysts reading faint signals and guessing intentions, with war one wrong guess away.

  2. 02

    Clancy made technical detail a pleasure, treating sonar, doctrine, and logistics as part of the drama rather than background.

  3. 03

    Jack Ryan wins by being an analyst rather than an action hero, a reminder that thinking clearly is its own kind of courage.
