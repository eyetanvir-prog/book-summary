Ready Player One

A treasure hunt inside a giant virtual world, built for readers who can name every 1980s game and film it references.

Ernest ClineFirst published 2008About 462 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    The OASIS is an escape from a ruined real world, and the book keeps asking whether escape is a solution or just a better-looking trap.

  2. 02

    Its central fight is over who owns a shared online space: a creative community of players or a corporation that wants to monetize every corner.

  3. 03

    The wall-to-wall nostalgia is both the fun and the weakness; when references stand in for character, the story can feel like a checklist.

Wade's devoted his life to studying the puzzles hidden within this world's digital confines--puzzles that are based on their creator's obsession with the pop culture of decades past and that promise massive power and fortune to whoever can unlock them.
