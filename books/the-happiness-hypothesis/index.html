The Happiness Hypothesis

Haidt tests ten ancient ideas about happiness against modern psychology and keeps the ones that survive.

Jonathan HaidtFirst published 2005About 317 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    The mind is like a rider on an elephant: reasoning steers a little, but emotion and habit do most of the moving.

  2. 02

    Happiness comes partly from getting the right relationship between yourself and others, work, and something larger, not from circumstances alone.

  3. 03

    Adversity can produce growth, but only under certain conditions, so the book is careful not to romanticize suffering.
