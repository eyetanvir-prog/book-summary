The Charisma Myth

Olivia Fox Cabane argues that charisma is a set of learnable behaviors rather than a fixed personality trait.

Olivia Fox CabaneFirst published 2012About 272 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Charisma comes from presence, power, and warmth, and people read all three from tiny cues in body language and attention.

  2. 02

    Your internal state leaks into your behavior, so managing anxiety and self-criticism matters as much as practicing outward techniques.

  3. 03

    Different charisma styles — focus, visionary, kindness, authority — fit different people and situations, so you can choose what suits you.
