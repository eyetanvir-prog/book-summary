Moralia

Plutarch’s sprawling essay collection covers anger, friendship, flattery, and animals with the curiosity of an ancient polymath.

PlutarchFirst published 1500About 301 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Many essays are practical ethics: how to tell a flatterer from a friend, how to control anger, how to benefit from your enemies.

  2. 02

    Plutarch mixes philosophy with anecdotes and customs, so the collection doubles as a window on everyday Greek and Roman thinking.

  3. 03

    It is uneven by design, so dip into individual essays that match your questions rather than reading straight through.
