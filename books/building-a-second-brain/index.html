Building a Second Brain

A practical system for capturing, organizing, and reusing notes and ideas so they become creative output.

Tiago ForteFirst published 2022About 240 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Forte’s CODE method means capture, organize, distill, and express, turning scattered information into usable knowledge.

  2. 02

    Organize notes by actionability with PARA, which stands for projects, areas, resources, and archives, rather than by subject.

  3. 03

    The point is not hoarding information but producing work, so build toward finished projects and share them.
