Sophie's World

A novel that teaches the history of Western philosophy through letters sent to a curious Norwegian teenager.

Jostein GaarderFirst published 1991About 523 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Gaarder uses a mystery frame to keep readers moving through ideas from the ancient Greeks to modern thinkers in order.

  2. 02

    The book’s real subject is wonder: philosophy begins when you stop taking the world for granted and ask why anything exists.

  3. 03

    As a novel it is sometimes stiff, but as a gentle introduction to big questions it works for teenagers and adults alike.
