Iron Curtain

How the Soviet Union imposed communism on Eastern Europe after 1945, and how people adapted or resisted.

Anne ApplebaumFirst published 2012About 627 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Applebaum shows that takeover was deliberate and fast: secret police, control of radio, and destruction of independent organisations came first.

  2. 02

    Totalitarian systems aimed to eliminate civil society, from youth groups to churches, because independent associations threatened total control.

  3. 03

    She documents the choices of ordinary people, collaboration, quiet resistance, and accommodation, without simplifying them into heroes and villains.
