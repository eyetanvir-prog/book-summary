Destroy Me

A short Shatter Me novella that switches to Warner’s point of view, reframing the villain readers thought they understood.

Tahereh MafiFirst published 2012About 111 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Seeing events from Warner’s mind shows how much of his coldness is armour against a cruel father and a brutal regime.

  2. 02

    It deepens the series’ central question of whether someone raised inside oppression can choose to become someone better.

  3. 03

    It is a bridge for fans rather than a standalone story, and it works best read between the first two main novels.
