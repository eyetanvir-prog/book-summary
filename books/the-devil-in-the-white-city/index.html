The Devil in the White City

Two true stories from Chicago in 1893: the men building a dazzling World’s Fair and a killer working in its shadow.

Erik LarsonFirst published 2003About 496 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Larson sets the architect Daniel Burnham’s near-impossible deadline against H. H. Holmes’s predation, showing ambition and menace growing from the same boomtown.

  2. 02

    The fair itself becomes a character, standing for America’s confidence in engineering, electricity, and spectacle at the turn of the century.

  3. 03

    His novelistic style makes history read fast, though some scenes inside Holmes’s head rely on inference more than documents.
