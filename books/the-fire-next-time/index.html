The Fire Next Time

Two letters from James Baldwin on race, faith and America that remain among the most urgent essays on the subject.

James BaldwinFirst published 1962About 120 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Baldwin argues that white Americans’ refusal to face their history traps everyone, and that honesty is a precondition for any real freedom.

  2. 02

    His account of the church and the Nation of Islam examines how faith can offer refuge and power while also enclosing people.

  3. 03

    Love, in Baldwin’s sense, is demanding and clear-eyed, the willingness to see people fully rather than a sentimental feeling.
