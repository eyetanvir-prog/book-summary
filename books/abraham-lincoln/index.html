Abraham Lincoln

A poet’s lyrical, sprawling biography of Lincoln, condensed from his famous multi-volume work.

Carl SandburgFirst published 1926About 640 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Sandburg roots Lincoln in frontier life and prairie culture, emphasising how his humble origins shaped his language and instincts.

  2. 02

    The writing is literary and reverent, closer to an American epic than to a critical, source-driven academic biography.

  3. 03

    It is valuable for how it shaped Lincoln’s popular image, though modern historians have corrected many of its romantic details.
