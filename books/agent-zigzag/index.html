Agent Zigzag

The true story of a charming British crook who became a double agent working for both sides in World War II.

Ben MacintyreFirst published 2001About 372 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Eddie Chapman was recruited by the Germans as a saboteur, then offered his services to British intelligence instead.

  2. 02

    Macintyre uses declassified files to show how spymasters on both sides managed, trusted, and manipulated an unreliable but valuable agent.

  3. 03

    The book explores loyalty as something negotiated and shifting, with Chapman attached more to people than to countries or causes.
