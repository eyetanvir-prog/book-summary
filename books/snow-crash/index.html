Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Stephenson imagined the Metaverse early, and the book still shapes how tech people talk about virtual worlds.

  2. 02

    Its big idea links language, religion, and code, suggesting that minds can be hacked like operating systems.

  3. 03

    The tone is gleefully satirical about corporate states and franchise culture, though long info-dumps interrupt the action.

Hiro's friend and fellow hacker Da5id views a bitmap image contained in the file which causes his computer to crash and Da5id to suffer brain damage in the real world. This is the future we now live where all can be brought to life in the metaverse and now all can be taken away.
