The Clear Quran

A modern English translation designed for clarity, aiming to be faithful to the Arabic and easy for today’s readers.

Mustafa KhattabFirst published 2016About 375 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Khattab uses contemporary, fluent English, avoiding archaic phrasing so that the meaning comes through directly for first-time readers.

  2. 02

    Brief footnotes and introductions to each sura provide context without overwhelming the text with commentary.

  3. 03

    It has become popular with students and converts, and pairs well with a more literal translation when studying specific wording.
