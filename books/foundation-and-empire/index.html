Foundation and Empire

The second Foundation novel tests Hari Seldon’s plan against a dying empire and an enemy no mathematics predicted.

Isaac AsimovFirst published 1945About 253 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Asimov asks what happens to statistical predictions when a single unexpected individual can change history’s course.

  2. 02

    The Foundation’s confidence in Seldon’s plan becomes a weakness, since faith in inevitability breeds complacency.

  3. 03

    It is a stronger, more suspenseful book than the first, with a central mystery worth avoiding spoilers on.
