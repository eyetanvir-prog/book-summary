Everything Is F*cked

Mark Manson’s irreverent look at why we feel hopeless amid material progress, and where hope comes from.

Mark MansonFirst published 2019About 288 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Manson argues hope requires values, community, and a sense of control, and losing those breeds despair.

  2. 02

    Emotions drive most decisions, so understanding the “feeling brain” matters more than relying on reason.

  3. 03

    Modern consumer culture and technology sell fake freedom, while real freedom involves limits and responsibility.
