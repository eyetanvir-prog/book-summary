Pretty Girls

Two estranged sisters, still marked by their teenage sister’s disappearance, reunite after a sudden death exposes something terrible.

Karin SlaughterFirst published 2001About 483 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Slaughter examines how grief can split a family, with each sister coping through opposite lives and choices.

  2. 02

    It is extremely violent and explicit, so it suits readers who can handle graphic depictions of abuse.

  3. 03

    The core theme is truth versus comfort, and the sisters must decide how much they are willing to know.
