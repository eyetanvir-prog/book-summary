With the Old Breed

A Marine’s unflinching memoir of Peleliu and Okinawa, among the most brutal battles of the Pacific War.

E.B. SledgeFirst published 2007About 368 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Sledge describes combat in physical detail, mud, rot, exhaustion, and fear, stripping away any glamour from war.

  2. 02

    He shows how prolonged violence changed men, including himself, eroding ordinary moral restraints toward the enemy and the dead.

  3. 03

    Written decades later from notes he kept, it combines immediacy with reflection, which is why historians and soldiers rate it so highly.
