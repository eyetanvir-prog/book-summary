The Bluest Eye

Toni Morrison’s first novel follows a Black girl in 1940s Ohio who prays for blue eyes, believing they would make her loved.

Toni MorrisonFirst published 1970About 181 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Morrison shows how white beauty standards get absorbed into families and communities until children learn to hate themselves.

  2. 02

    The book refuses a single villain, tracing harm through layers of poverty, humiliation, and inherited pain.

  3. 03

    Its fractured structure and seasonal sections force readers to piece the story together, mirroring a community that looked away.
