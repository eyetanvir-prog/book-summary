Peter the Great

The giant, restless tsar who dragged Russia toward Europe by force of will, and at enormous human cost.

Robert K. MassieFirst published 1980About 921 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Peter travelled west to learn shipbuilding and statecraft himself, then imposed Western dress, institutions, and a new capital on a reluctant country.

  2. 02

    His reforms were driven by war with Sweden; modernisation served military power, which explains both his successes and his brutality.

  3. 03

    Massie captures Peter’s contradictions, curious and practical yet capable of torturing his own son, without letting either side cancel the other.
