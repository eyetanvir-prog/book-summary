Dominion

Tom Holland argues that even secular Western values are, at root, Christian inheritances most people no longer notice.

Tom HollandFirst published 2019About 624 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Holland traces ideas like human equality, concern for the weak, and individual conscience back to the scandal of a god dying on a cross.

  2. 02

    He follows these ideas through saints, reformers, revolutionaries, and modern activists who often reject Christianity while relying on its assumptions.

  3. 03

    The argument is sweeping and sometimes selective, but it makes a strong case that Western morality is not simply universal common sense.

Open Library note. Christianity is the most enduring and influential legacy of the ancient world, and its emergence the single most transformative development in Western history. Even the increasing number in the West today who have abandoned the faith of their forebears, and dismiss all religion as pointless superstition, remain recognisably its heirs. Seen close-up, the division between a sceptic and a believer may seem unbridgeable.
