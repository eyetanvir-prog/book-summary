The Warren Buffet Way

Robert Hagstrom distills Warren Buffett’s investing approach into principles for evaluating businesses and managing risk.

Robert G. HagstromFirst published 1994About 313 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Buffett buys businesses, not just stocks, focusing on durable economics, competent management, and sensible prices.

  2. 02

    Patience and concentration matter: fewer well-understood investments may beat constant trading and diversification for its own sake.

  3. 03

    Applying Buffett’s methods is harder than describing them, since judgment, temperament, and access shape outcomes.
