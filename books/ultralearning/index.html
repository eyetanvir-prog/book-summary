Ultralearning

A guide to aggressive, self-directed learning, inspired by the author’s MIT curriculum challenge.

Scott YoungFirst published 2019About 304 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Young describes ultralearning as intense, self-directed projects to acquire skills quickly and deeply.

  2. 02

    Principles include directness, drilling weak points, retrieval practice, and seeking honest feedback.

  3. 03

    Designing projects around real-world application helps knowledge stick and transfer to actual tasks.

Multi-talented author, programmer and entrepreneur Scott Young has learnt four new languages and taught himself the entire four-year MIT curriculum for computer science all within a year. How? By using these seven life-changing ultralearning techniques designed for people, like him, who want to stand apart. Learning has never been easy, perhaps, because it has never been made easy.
