Ego Is the Enemy

A Stoic-flavored warning that your own ego sabotages you while aspiring, after success, and in failure alike.

Ryan HolidayFirst published 2016About 238 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    When aspiring, ego swaps work for talk and image, so stay a student and do the unglamorous work.

  2. 02

    Success breeds entitlement and paranoia, so manage yourself, keep perspective, and remember how much luck and help were involved.

  3. 03

    In failure, ego tells you that you deserve better, but the humble response is to keep working and learning.

Open Library note. Many of us insist the main impediment to a full, successful life is the outside world. In fact, the most common enemy lies within: our ego. Early in our careers, it impedes learning and the cultivation of talent. With success, it can blind us to our faults and sow future problems. In failure, it magnifies each blow and makes recovery more difficult. At every stage, ego holds us back. Ego Is the Enemy draws on a vast array of stories and examples, from literature to philosophy to history.
