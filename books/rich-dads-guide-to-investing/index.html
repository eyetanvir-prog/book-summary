Rich Dad's Guide to Investing

Kiyosaki explains what he believes the rich invest in and how ordinary investors can think more like them.

Robert T. KiyosakiFirst published 2000About 406 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    The book argues the best opportunities come from building businesses and assets, not from buying whatever investment products are sold to the public.

  2. 02

    Financial education, a clear plan, and control over your investments matter more than income for building lasting wealth.

  3. 03

    Its advice leans on aggressive, leveraged strategies and anecdote, so readers should weigh the risks carefully before acting on them.
