What If?

Randall Munroe answers absurd hypothetical questions with real physics, careful maths, and stick-figure jokes.

Randall MunroeFirst published 1980About 306 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Taking silly questions seriously is a good way to learn real science, because answering them requires calculating rather than guessing.

  2. 02

    Munroe shows how estimation and rough numbers can quickly reveal whether an idea is plausible, impossible, or catastrophic.

  3. 03

    The book treats curiosity as worthwhile for its own sake, even when the answer is that everyone involved would immediately die.
