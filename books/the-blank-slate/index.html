The Blank Slate

Pinker argues that denying human nature distorts politics, science, and parenting, and that evidence for it is not dangerous.

Steven PinkerFirst published 2002About 528 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    The mind is not infinitely malleable; genes and evolved tendencies shape temperament, abilities, and behavior alongside culture.

  2. 02

    Admitting innate differences need not justify inequality or fatalism, he argues, because moral equality does not depend on biological sameness.

  3. 03

    Parenting may matter less for adult personality than many assume, a claim that remains debated and deserves a careful reader.
