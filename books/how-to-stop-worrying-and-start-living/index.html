How to Stop Worrying and Start Living

Carnegie’s practical companion on worry, built from anecdotes and simple rules for getting anxiety down to manageable size.

Dale CarnegieFirst published 1936About 348 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Live in “day-tight compartments”: focus on today’s work rather than dwelling on yesterday’s regrets or tomorrow’s fears.

  2. 02

    Ask what the worst realistic outcome is, accept it, and then calmly work to improve on it.

  3. 03

    Keep busy, check the actual odds of your fears, and refuse to fret over small things or what cannot be changed.
