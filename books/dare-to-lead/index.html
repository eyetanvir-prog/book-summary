Dare to Lead

Brené Brown applies her research on vulnerability and shame to the everyday work of leading teams.

Brené BrownFirst published 2018About 320 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Courageous leadership requires vulnerability — showing up when outcomes are uncertain — rather than armoring up against risk.

  2. 02

    Clear is kind: avoiding hard conversations to stay nice leaves people confused and creates resentment later.

  3. 03

    Living into stated values and building trust through small actions make teams willing to take risks and give honest feedback.

Open Library note. Leadership is not about titles, status, and wielding power. A leader is anyone who takes responsibility for recognizing the potential in people and ideas, and has the courage to develop that potential. When we dare to lead, we don’t pretend to have the right answers; we stay curious and ask the right questions. We don’t see power as finite and hoard it; we know that power becomes infinite when we share it with others.
