The Color of Law

A historian shows that American residential segregation was built deliberately by federal, state, and local governments, not by accident.

Richard RothsteinFirst published 2017About 345 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Public housing, federal mortgage underwriting, zoning, and highway placement were used explicitly to separate Black and white neighborhoods throughout the twentieth century.

  2. 02

    Rothstein argues that because segregation was government policy, it violates the Constitution, which means remedies are an obligation rather than an act of charity.

  3. 03

    Locked-out home ownership compounded across generations, which helps explain today’s racial wealth gap even where explicit discrimination has formally ended.
