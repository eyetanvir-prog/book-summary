Seven Brief Lessons on Physics

Carlo Rovelli explains modern physics in seven short, elegant lessons that fit into a single sitting.

Carlo RovelliFirst published 2014About 88 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Rovelli introduces general relativity, quantum mechanics, cosmology, particles, and quantum gravity in clear, poetic language.

  2. 02

    He emphasises that physics reveals a world very different from everyday intuition, where space and time are dynamic.

  3. 03

    The final lesson places humans within nature, suggesting that scientific understanding is part of what makes us human.
