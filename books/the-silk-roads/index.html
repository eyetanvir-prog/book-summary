The Silk Roads

Peter Frankopan retells world history from the trade routes that connected East and West across Central Asia.

Peter FrankopanFirst published 2015About 128 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    The regions linking Europe and Asia have been the center of world history, not a periphery to the West.

  2. 02

    Trade along the Silk Roads spread goods, religions, diseases, and ideas, shaping empires across continents.

  3. 03

    The book challenges Western-centered narratives, though its vast scope means some topics receive only brief treatment.
