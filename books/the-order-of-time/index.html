The Order of Time

Rovelli argues that physics has dismantled our everyday idea of time, then asks why it still feels like it flows.

Carlo RovelliFirst published 2017About 234 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    There is no single universal now; time passes at different rates depending on gravity and motion, so simultaneity across distance is undefined.

  2. 02

    Rovelli argues the arrow of time is tied to entropy and our blurred, partial view of the world, not built into the basic laws.

  3. 03

    The felt flow of time may come from memory and anticipation, making it as much about us as about the universe.
