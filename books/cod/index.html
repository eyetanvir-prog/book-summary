Cod

The fish that fed empires, launched colonies, and was nearly fished out of existence by modern fleets.

Mark KurlanskyFirst published 1997About 304 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Salted cod was a durable, tradable protein that powered European expansion, Basque fortunes, and the economies of New England and Newfoundland.

  2. 02

    Fishing nations fought repeatedly over cod, including the Cod Wars between Britain and Iceland, showing how a resource shapes national identity.

  3. 03

    The collapse of the Grand Banks fishery shows that apparently endless abundance can disappear quickly once technology outruns restraint.

Wars have been fought over it, revolutions have been spurred by it, national diets have been founded on it, economies and livelihoods have depended on it, the settlement of North America was based on it. To those it has sustained, it is a treasure more precious than gold. It is the codfish, whose story – concisely and eloquently told by Mark Kurlansky – casts a fascinating and revealing fight on world history.COD spans a thousand years and four continents.
