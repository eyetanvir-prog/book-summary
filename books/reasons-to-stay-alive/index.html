Reasons to Stay Alive

Matt Haig writes about severe depression and anxiety, offering hope through honesty, small steps, and survival.

Matt HaigFirst published 2015About 272 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Haig describes depression from the inside, helping readers understand that it is not simply sadness or weakness.

  2. 02

    Recovery is uneven; small things like reading, walking, and connection can help without offering instant cure.

  3. 03

    The book’s main message is that pain can lie about the future, so staying alive gives change a chance.

Open Library note. "Like nearly one in five people, Matt Haig suffers from depression. Reasons to Stay Alive is Matt’s inspiring account of how, minute by minute and day by day, he overcame the disease with the help of reading, writing, and the love of his parents and his girlfriend (and now-wife), Andrea. And eventually, he learned to appreciate life all the more for it."
