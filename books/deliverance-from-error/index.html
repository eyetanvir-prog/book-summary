Deliverance from Error

A brilliant scholar describes losing his certainty, quitting a prestigious post, and finding his way back through lived spiritual practice.

Al-GhazaliFirst published 1980About 334 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Al-Ghazali tests each route to knowledge — theology, philosophy, the claims of secret teachers — and finds each one useful but unable to give him certainty alone.

  2. 02

    His breakdown is the hinge: reputation and career could not quiet his doubts, and he chose to walk away from them to seek sincerity.

  3. 03

    He concludes that Sufi practice offers direct taste of truth that argument cannot supply, while still insisting on reason’s proper limits and the Shariah.
