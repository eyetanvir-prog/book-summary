The House of Wisdom

Jonathan Lyons tells how Arab scholarship in astronomy, medicine, and philosophy flowed into medieval Europe and shaped the West.

Jonathan LyonsFirst published 2009About 260 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Baghdad’s translation movement preserved and expanded Greek and Persian learning, producing original advances in mathematics, astronomy, and medicine.

  2. 02

    European scholars like Adelard of Bath traveled to learn Arabic science, carrying knowledge that fed the later scientific revolution.

  3. 03

    Lyons argues the West has downplayed this debt, a corrective that is valuable even when his narrative leans toward advocacy.
