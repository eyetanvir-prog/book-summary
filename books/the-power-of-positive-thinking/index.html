The Power of Positive Thinking

A 1950s Christian self-help bestseller pairing faith, prayer, and optimism as a route to confidence and peace of mind.

Norman Vincent PealeFirst published 1952About 276 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Peale urges replacing defeatist thoughts with faith-filled affirmations, picturing success, and believing in yourself through belief in God.

  2. 02

    Practical habits such as quiet time, prayer, and refusing to dwell on worries are meant to reduce stress and increase energy.

  3. 03

    Psychologists and clergy criticized its anecdotes and its risk of encouraging denial, so read it as devotional encouragement rather than science.
