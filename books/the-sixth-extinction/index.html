The Sixth Extinction

Elizabeth Kolbert reports from the field on how humans are driving a mass extinction comparable to the dinosaurs’.

Elizabeth KolbertFirst published 2014About 352 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Kolbert shows earlier mass extinctions were caused by natural catastrophes, while this one is driven mostly by human activity.

  2. 02

    Habitat destruction, climate change, invasive species, and ocean acidification are reshaping the planet’s living systems.

  3. 03

    Each chapter centres on a specific species, making a global crisis concrete through individual stories of decline and loss.
