Just Kids

Patti Smith remembers her friendship with Robert Mapplethorpe and their struggle to become artists in New York.

Patti SmithFirst published 2006About 320 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Smith shows art-making as devotion, poverty, and daily practice before success arrives, if it ever does.

  2. 02

    Her bond with Mapplethorpe changes but endures, presenting love and friendship as more complex than labels.

  3. 03

    The book captures a lost New York of cheap rooms, artistic risk, and chance encounters with emerging artists.

Open Library note. In this memoir, singer-songwriter Patti Smith shares tales of New York City : the denizens of Max's Kansas City, the Hotel Chelsea, Scribner's, Brentano's and Strand bookstores and her new life in Brooklyn with a young man named Robert Mapplethorpe--the man who changed her life with his love, friendship, and genius.
