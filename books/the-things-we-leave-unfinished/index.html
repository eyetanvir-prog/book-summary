The Things We Leave Unfinished

A woman hires a famous author to finish her late grandmother’s last novel, and two love stories run side by side.

Rebecca YarrosFirst published 2021About 424 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Yarros alternates between a present-day romance and a Second World War story, linking grief across generations.

  2. 02

    The question of how a story should end becomes a debate about honesty, happiness, and what readers deserve.

  3. 03

    It is emotional and romance-driven, best for readers who enjoy dual timelines and big feelings.
