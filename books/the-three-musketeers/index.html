The Three Musketeers

Swordfights, court intrigue and a young Gascon’s ambitions make Dumas’s adventure novel as fast and funny as its reputation suggests.

Alexandre DumasFirst published 1844About 555 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Friendship and loyalty drive the story, but Dumas also lets his heroes be vain, broke, reckless and sometimes morally questionable.

  2. 02

    Behind the duels lies real political rivalry between crown and cardinal, giving history a playful, gossip-filled serial treatment.

  3. 03

    Written in serial instalments, the book is built around constant cliffhangers, so its pace still works for modern readers who want momentum.
