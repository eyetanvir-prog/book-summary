Blood of Elves

The first full Witcher novel slows the monster-hunting down to watch Geralt, a sorceress and a hunted princess become a strange family.

Andrzej SapkowskiFirst published 1994About 379 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Sapkowski is more interested in politics, prejudice and refugees than in battles; elves and dwarves are minorities with real grievances.

  2. 02

    The heart of the book is Ciri’s training, and the question of who gets to shape a powerful child — guardians, kings or prophecy.

  3. 03

    Expect long conversations and shifting viewpoints rather than a tidy quest; it reads like a prologue to the larger war to come.
