Start with Why

Sinek’s argument that people follow leaders and buy from companies because of what they believe, not what they sell.

Simon SinekFirst published 2009About 246 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    The Golden Circle runs from why to how to what; inspiring organizations communicate their purpose first while most start with product features.

  2. 02

    Loyalty comes from shared belief, so manipulations like discounts and promotions buy transactions but rarely build long-term trust or devotion.

  3. 03

    Its neuroscience framing and chosen examples are tidier than the evidence, but the practical prompt — articulate your purpose clearly — still sharpens messaging.
