Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Define what you actually want, then calculate its cost, since many dreams are cheaper than people assume.

  2. 02

    Cut work using the eighty-twenty principle, batch low-value tasks, and outsource or eliminate everything else.

  3. 03

    Some advice depends on online businesses and remote work arrangements that are less easy than described, but the questions it asks endure.
