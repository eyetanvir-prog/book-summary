Understanding Power

Edited transcripts of Noam Chomsky’s talks and discussions, laying out his critique of media, foreign policy and corporate power.

Noam ChomskyFirst published 2002About 432 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Chomsky argues that media and institutions shape public opinion within narrow limits, protecting elite interests while appearing free.

  2. 02

    He examines US foreign policy through case studies, insisting it should be judged by the same standards applied to enemies.

  3. 03

    Its extensive online footnotes support claims, but the book is a sustained argument, so read it critically like any polemic.
