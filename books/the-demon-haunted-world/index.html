The Demon-Haunted World

Carl Sagan’s defence of scientific thinking as a candle against superstition, pseudoscience, and easy deception.

Carl SaganFirst published 1995About 472 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Sagan offers a ‘baloney detection kit’, practical tools for testing claims, such as seeking independent confirmation and considering alternative explanations.

  2. 02

    He examines alien abductions, witch hunts, and faith healing to show how human perception and wishful thinking can mislead us.

  3. 03

    He warns that a society dependent on science yet ignorant of it becomes vulnerable to manipulation, a warning that now reads as prophecy.
