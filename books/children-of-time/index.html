Children of Time

Humanity’s last survivors seek a terraformed planet, but an experiment there has produced a new intelligent species.

Adrian TchaikovskyFirst published 2015About 616 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Tchaikovsky imagines a non-human civilisation evolving in detail, showing how biology shapes culture, communication, and science.

  2. 02

    The parallel human story shows a species repeating old mistakes, contrasting decline with another’s ascent.

  3. 03

    It argues that understanding minds unlike ours is the real test of intelligence, not technology alone.
