Three ideas to keep

    Bartlett argues that small daily standards and consistent habits matter more than motivation or grand plans for long-term results.

  2. 02

    He stresses telling stories well and understanding psychology, since persuasion and attention drive success in business and media.

  3. 03

    The laws are brisk and quotable, but some rest on anecdote and podcast wisdom, so test them against your own context.
