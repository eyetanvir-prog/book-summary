Then She Was Gone

Ten years after her teenage daughter disappears, a grieving mother meets a charming man whose young child seems unsettlingly familiar.

Lisa JewellFirst published 2017About 384 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Jewell is most interested in grief’s long tail: how a family fractures, and how a mother keeps living around an absence.

  2. 02

    Multiple viewpoints, including past and present, let the reader see more than Laurel does, which builds dread rather than surprise.

  3. 03

    It is a domestic thriller with dark material, more about emotional aftermath than detective work.
