A History of the Arab Peoples

Albert Hourani’s authoritative history of the Arabic-speaking world from the rise of Islam to the late twentieth century.

Albert HouraniFirst published 1991About 624 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Hourani emphasizes continuity in cities, scholarship, and law, showing a shared Arab-Islamic culture that persisted across changing dynasties.

  2. 02

    He gives as much attention to social life — trade, family, learning — as to rulers and wars, making the history feel inhabited.

  3. 03

    Dense but measured, it remains a benchmark survey, though the final chapters reflect the period before more recent upheavals.
