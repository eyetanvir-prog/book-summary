Influence

The field guide to the mental shortcuts that make you say yes, written by a psychologist who went undercover among salespeople.

Robert B. CialdiniFirst published 1983About 287 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Six levers — reciprocity, commitment and consistency, social proof, liking, authority, and scarcity — trigger automatic compliance because they usually serve us well as shortcuts.

  2. 02

    Small initial commitments, especially written or public ones, pull people toward larger later ones because we hate looking inconsistent to ourselves.

  3. 03

    The defense is not avoiding shortcuts but noticing when someone has manufactured the cue, such as fake scarcity or a gift designed to create debt.
