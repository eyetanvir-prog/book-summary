    Radio becomes the novel’s moral wire, carrying both lessons that open the mind and propaganda that shuts it down.

    Short alternating chapters make it very readable, though some critics find the prose more polished than the history it covers.

Open Library note. From the highly acclaimed, multiple award-winning Anthony Doerr, a stunningly ambitious and beautiful novel about a blind French girl and a German boy whose paths collide in occupied France as both try to survive the devastation of World War II. Marie Laure lives with her father in Paris within walking distance of the Museum of Natural History where he works as the master of the locks (there are thousands of locks in the museum).
