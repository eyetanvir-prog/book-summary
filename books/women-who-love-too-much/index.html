Women Who Love Too Much

Robin Norwood describes a pattern of women drawn to unavailable or damaging partners and offers a route toward recovery.

Robin NorwoodFirst published 1985About 302 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Loving too much often begins in families with addiction or emotional neglect, making chaos and rescuing feel familiar and normal.

  2. 02

    Norwood treats the pattern like an addiction, encouraging support groups and focusing on one’s own healing rather than changing the partner.

  3. 03

    Its framework is dated and narrow, but readers still find its descriptions recognisable and useful as a starting point.
