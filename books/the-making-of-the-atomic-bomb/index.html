The Making of the Atomic Bomb

Richard Rhodes traces the science, people, and politics behind the creation of the first nuclear weapons.

Richard RhodesFirst published 1986About 886 pagesCard · 1 min

Three ideas to keep

  1. 01

    Decades of physics discoveries about the atom made the bomb possible before anyone fully grasped its implications.

  2. 02

    Scientists like Szilard, Oppenheimer, and Fermi wrestled with moral questions while racing to build the weapon during wartime.

  3. 03

    The book shows how scientific knowledge, state power, and war combined to create a technology that changed global politics.

Open Library note. Here for the first time, in rich, human, political, and scientific detail, is the complete story of how the bomb was developed, from the turn-of-the-century discovery of the vast energy locked inside the atom to the dropping of the first bombs on Japan. Few great discoveries have evolved so swiftly -- or have been so misunderstood. From the theoretical discussions of nuclear energy to the bright glare of Trinity there was a span of hardly more than twenty-five years.
